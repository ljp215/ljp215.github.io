AI 研究/Benchmark XAaron Levie

Now that the base open weights AI models are getting far better, and post training infra is becoming more mature and commercialized, there are going to be all new plays for companies that have large amounts of data to have their own models. Licensing data for external model training was previously the only play if you had a large corpus of information, but you can now reasonably go and train your own models as well without incurring the cost and complexity of competing with the labs on research. The general purpose frontier models will still have a leg up in broad areas due to the ability to handle the widest set of tasks, but you can absolutely see a future where we have far more models than today across every vertical and domain.

译文如今，基础开放权重AI模型正变得越来越好，后训练基础设施也日趋成熟和商业化，拥有大量数据的公司将迎来全新玩法，可以打造自己的模型。 过去，如果你拥有大规模语料，授权数据用于外部模型训练是唯一选择；但现在，你也可以合理地去训练自己的模型，而无需承担与实验室在科研上竞争所需的成本和复杂性。 通用前沿模型在广泛领域仍将占据优势，因为它们能处理最多样的任务，但你完全可以看到这样一个未来：每个垂直领域和行业中，模型数量将远超今天。

预计阅读 2 分钟

AI 研究/Benchmark XMadhu Guru

If you’re a PM, there is immense alpha in understanding the model frontier for your specific product and use cases. You should be able to answer the following better than most frontier lab researchers and PMs for your use cases: 1/ What can models in each size category do well today? 2/ Where does each model fail? 3/ What workarounds can you use to overcome these failures ? 4/ Given the trajectory, what will these models likely be able to do 2–3 months from now? How should your roadmap look given this? This is a core part of the PM job now.

译文如果你是产品经理，理解模型前沿对于你的特定产品和用例有着巨大的价值。 你应该能够比大多数前沿实验室的研究人员和产品经理更好地回答以下问题： 1/ 目前各尺寸类别的模型擅长做什么？ 2/ 每个模型在哪些方面会失败？ 3/ 你可以用什么变通方法克服这些失败？ 4/ 根据发展趋势，这些模型在2-3个月后可能能够做什么？你的路线图应如何据此调整？ 这现在是产品经理工作的核心部分。
