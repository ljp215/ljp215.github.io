AI 公司/行业动态 XAaron Levie

The rate of progress in AI right now is unlike any other period in tech history. Models are getting cheaper on a like for like task basis, more generally capable, faster, and they’re going deeper in almost every domain. As intelligence becomes too cheap to meter, then the big opportunity is driving diffusion of AI into the economy. This is a great time to be applied AI company because there’s an incredible amount of innovation and competition acting as a tailwind to your own progress. Huge moment for startups right now that can take advantage of this.

译文当前AI的发展速度在科技史上前所未有。模型在同类任务上的成本越来越低，通用能力越来越强，速度越来越快，并且几乎在每个领域都深入发展。 当智能变得便宜到无需计量时，最大的机会就是将AI扩散到经济中。 现在是成为应用型AI公司的大好时机，因为大量的创新和竞争会成为你进步的顺风。对于能够利用这一点的初创企业来说，现在是一个巨大的时刻。
