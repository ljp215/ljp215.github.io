AI 研究/Benchmark XAaron Levie

Correct take. 99% of tokens in the world will get consumed in an enterprise context. They will be for writing code, combing through insane amounts of life sciences research, automating manufacturing, securing enterprises, detecting fraud, and thousands of other tasks like this that have significant economic value. This is both where the cost of AI can be most justified but also where the impact of parallel workers doing tasks on your behalf is most useful. Even the agents that most impact consumers will often be wrapped up as just end to end services where the consumer doesn’t think about there being an AI system on the other end, like faster insurance or onboarding to a bank. The key though is it’s going to take years for diffusion of agents across the economy because workflows have to re-engineered to incorporate AI, which is a complete change to how most work happens today. Anyone who’s expecting this to happen overnight should be updating their timelines.

译文正确的观点。世界上99%的token将在企业环境中被消耗。 它们将用于编写代码、梳理海量的生命科学研究、自动化制造、保护企业安全、检测欺诈，以及成千上万其他具有显著经济价值的任务。这既是AI成本最能被合理化的地方，也是并行工作者代表你执行任务时影响最大的地方。 即使是那些对消费者影响最大的智能体，也往往会被包装成端到端的服务，消费者不会想到另一端有AI系统，比如更快的保险或银行开户。 但关键在于，智能体在经济中的普及需要数年时间，因为工作流程必须重新设计以融入AI，这是对当今大多数工作方式的彻底改变。任何期望这一切在一夜之间发生的人都应该更新他们的时间表。
