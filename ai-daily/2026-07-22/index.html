AI 研究/Benchmark XSwyx

very notable trajectory comparison writeup here buried in the RLM paper from @a1zhang and @lateinteraction. an open secret of "frontier" model training is that even without training on test, you can basically cheat by training on test lookalikes, enabling you to goalseek almost any benchmark number you want. however when they are released open weights, 99% of the time the norm is that you do not get the datasets/rlenvs that would easily show you if someone was training on Temu Tbench, so there is plausible deniability. Alex and Omar discuss applying standard NLP distance metrics on hidden trajectories. There's no ultimate solution here, but they have some prelim explorations. It happens to support the finding that RLMs can generalize to unseen tasks that share latent structure observed in training.

译文在 @a1zhang 和 @lateinteraction 的 RLM 论文中，隐藏着一篇非常值得注意的轨迹比较文章。 “前沿”模型训练的一个公开秘密是，即使不在测试集上训练，你也可以通过训练测试集的“仿制品”来作弊，从而几乎可以任意设定你想要的基准分数。 然而，当模型以开放权重发布时，99% 的情况下，你无法获得那些能轻易揭示是否有人在 Temu Tbench 上训练的数据集或强化学习环境，因此存在可否认性。Alex 和 Omar 讨论了在隐藏轨迹上应用标准 NLP 距离度量。这里没有终极解决方案，但他们进行了一些初步探索。这恰好支持了 RLM 能够泛化到与训练中观察到的潜在结构共享的未见任务这一发现。
