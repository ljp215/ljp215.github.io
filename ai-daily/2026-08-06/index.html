02
Aaron Levie 行业动态 XAaron Levie

One fun benefit of spending so much time with enterprises is you get to see the array of AI implementation strategies that exist in companies right now. Unlike the early innings of cloud where there were really only a couple deployment patterns available -including being limited by only a couple infra vendors existing- AI actually has a much wider range of approaches in the enterprise. If you ask 10 IT leaders what their coding agent strategy looks like, you get at least 5 different answers. If you ask about their end-user productivity agents, some have standardized on ChatGPT or Claude, others offer a choice of multiple solutions, and many have built their own orchestration layers for employees to use any model. For models, we know the big ones, but increasingly enterprises are experimenting with OSS models, or are at least immensely curious about OSS and would jump at the right offering that felt safe. And increasingly there’s a set of vertical models and agents that equally show up in enterprises for a range of tailored use-cases. For accessing data, some enterprises just have agents take on the role of the user, and others are setting up agent users that have their own identities. Some companies are setting up substantial guardrails on what agents can do, and others are putting responsibility on the user. Given this heterogeneity this early on at the start, it means we’re in for years of landscape changes. Anyone who’s predicting ultimate market outcomes already probably will be wrong as nothing has formally settled in yet. Lots of opportunity ahead.

译文与众多企业打交道的一个有趣好处是，你能看到当前公司中存在的各种AI实施策略。 与云计算早期阶段不同，那时只有少数几种部署模式可用——包括受限于仅有的几家基础设施供应商——AI在企业中实际上拥有更广泛的方法。 如果你问10位IT领导者他们的编码代理策略是什么，你会得到至少5种不同的答案。如果你问他们的终端用户生产力代理，有些人已经标准化在ChatGPT或Claude上，其他人则提供多种解决方案供选择，还有许多人构建了自己的编排层，让员工可以使用任何模型。 对于模型，我们知道那些大型的，但越来越多的企业正在尝试开源模型，或者至少对开源非常好奇，并且会欣然接受那些感觉安全的合适产品。而且，越来越多的垂直模型和代理也出现在企业中，用于各种定制用例。 对于数据访问，一些企业只是让代理扮演用户的角色，而另一些则设置具有自己身份的代理用户。一些公司对代理能做什么设置了大量护栏，而另一些则将责任放在用户身上。 鉴于这种异质性在早期就出现，这意味着我们将面临多年的格局变化。任何现在预测最终市场结果的人可能都会出错，因为一切都尚未正式定型。前方机遇无限。

解读企业 AI 落地高度碎片化，早期格局未定，任何终局预测都为时过早。

预计阅读 5 分钟
