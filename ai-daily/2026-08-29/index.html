03
AI Agent/开发者基础设施 XAaron Levie

This week’s tech earnings calls were a critical reminder of how valuable the relationship is between software and AI. Software provides the guardrails for how data is managed, how business logic in workflows are maintained, how you manage and govern access to information, how you protect data, and more. You want these systems to work the same way every time, without fail. Agents, on the other hand, work inside these systems and with this data to execute on tasks just like people would have. But at a scale far greater than people ever did, which is especially why the deterministic controls matter more than ever. And in fact many of the best ways to deploy agents will be directly within the software systems themselves, like from Salesforce, Box, Harvey, ServiceNow, and so on. These platforms can optimize agents for their workflows and domains, with evals and context that are unique to their vertical, as well as connect to other agents headlessly. The net result is both software and AI adoption go up, hand in hand. Software and Agents are going to work together, and ultimately grow the size of the IT TAM dramatically as a result over time.

译文本周的科技财报电话会议提醒我们，软件与AI之间的关系有多么宝贵。 软件为数据管理方式、工作流中的业务逻辑维护、信息访问的管理与治理、数据保护等提供了保障。你希望这些系统每次都能以相同的方式运行，不出差错。 而代理（Agent）则在这些系统内部，利用这些数据，像人类一样执行任务。但规模远超人类所能，这也正是确定性控制比以往任何时候都更重要的原因。 事实上，许多最佳的代理部署方式将直接在软件系统本身中实现，比如Salesforce、Box、Harvey、ServiceNow等。这些平台可以针对其工作流和领域优化代理，利用其垂直领域独有的评估和上下文，还能以无头方式连接其他代理。 最终结果是软件和AI的采用率携手增长。软件和代理将协同工作，并最终随着时间的推移大幅扩大IT市场的总规模。

AI 研究/Benchmark XMadhu Guru

If you lead AI for an enterprise, the highest leverage thing you can do is to make your AI stack model agnostic. There are two things I would invest in. 1/ Today: build an eval suite that fully captures your use cases and business outcomes. Most companies I’ve seen do not do a good job of this. 2/ Within the next year: build the capability to post-train open models. The talent gap here is even more acute, so start planning today. This gives you the flexibility to switch models, customize them, compare them on your own workloads, and optimize for the best combination of quality, cost and latency. own the evals, own the models.

译文如果你负责企业的 AI，你能做的最具杠杆效应的事情就是让你的 AI 技术栈与模型无关。 我会投资两件事。 1/ 现在：构建一个能全面捕捉你的用例和业务成果的评估套件。我见过的大多数公司在这方面做得并不好。 2/ 在未来一年内：建立对开放模型进行后训练的能力。这里的人才缺口更为严重，所以今天就开始规划。 这会给你灵活性，让你可以切换模型、定制模型、在自己的工作负载上比较它们，并优化质量、成本和延迟的最佳组合。 掌控评估，掌控模型。
