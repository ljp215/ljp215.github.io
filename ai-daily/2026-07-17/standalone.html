OpenAI 行业动态 XPeter Yang

ChatGPT Live and Codex are two incredible products that don’t talk to each other. This is @OpenAI's biggest missed opportunity imo. I went on a walk with ChatGPT Live and asked it to pull up my Google Doc. It said it couldn’t. I then manually triggered the Documents plugin to find my Google Doc, and all of a sudden, ChatGPT Live had the right context. It would be amazing to talk to ChatGPT Live and have it be able to use all the plugins, tools, and browser use that Codex has access to. Then I could ask it to reply to emails, schedule meetings, edit docs, ship code, and more all during a live conversation. I think the first step is to make ChatGPT Live aware of all the plugins that it’s already connected to. Why build such a great voice assistant but have it not be able to do anything?

译文ChatGPT Live 和 Codex 是两个了不起的产品，但它们彼此之间无法沟通。 在我看来，这是 @OpenAI 最大的错失良机。 我带着 ChatGPT Live 散步，让它调出我的 Google 文档。它说做不到。 然后我手动触发了文档插件来找到我的 Google 文档，突然之间，ChatGPT Live 就有了正确的上下文。 如果能和 ChatGPT Live 对话，并且它能使用 Codex 能访问的所有插件、工具和浏览器功能，那就太棒了。这样我就可以在实时对话中让它回复邮件、安排会议、编辑文档、部署代码等等。 我认为第一步是让 ChatGPT Live 意识到它已经连接的所有插件。 为什么打造了如此出色的语音助手，却让它什么都做不了？

解读产品割裂暴露了 OpenAI 在 agent 整合上的短板，语音助手缺乏工具调用能力。
