Peter Yang 行业动态 XPeter Yang

AI has been great for my productivity, but I’m starting to recognize three dark patterns: 1. Becoming too lazy to read anything It's too easy to just read AI summaries over the original piece or let agents go wild changing my files without reviewing what they did. 2. Getting distracted by agents while I’m out It’s too easy to open ChatGPT or Claude on my phone while I’m out to give feedback my agents and feel “productive,” even when I’m supposed to be watching my kids or doing literally anything else. 3. Preferring to talk to the agent vs. a human This is a new pattern with ChatGPT Voice, I sometimes find myself preferring to brainstorm with the agent instead of the actual human in the room.

译文AI 极大地提升了我的生产力，但我开始注意到三种负面模式： 1. 变得懒得阅读任何东西 直接看 AI 对原文的摘要，或者让智能体随意修改我的文件而不审查它们做了什么，这太容易了。 2. 外出时被智能体分散注意力 在外出时，我很容易在手机上打开 ChatGPT 或 Claude 给智能体提供反馈，并感觉自己“高效”，即使我本应照看孩子或做其他任何事。 3. 更愿意与智能体对话而不是真人 这是 ChatGPT Voice 带来的一种新模式：我有时发现自己更愿意与智能体头脑风暴，而不是和房间里的真人交流。

解读AI 提升效率的副作用是重塑用户行为习惯，本质是认知与注意力管理问题，而非技术缺陷。

AI 研究/Benchmark XAaron Levie

Thought provoking post by Dwarkesh. In general - as AI gets more powerful - we should expect on the margin that inference goes toward the most economically useful task, and everything else gets priced out. This in theory in a very scarce environment would cause the cost of inference to skyrocket because those tasks would be far more valuable than today. But I don’t think it plays out in the way that’s laid out just simply due to market forces competing for inference demand as a way of continuing to drive down prices until capacity can catch up. There are too many model providers and infra players that want these workloads to cause the effect that’s proposed here. But will see!

译文Dwarkesh 发表了一篇引人深思的文章。总体而言——随着 AI 变得更强大——我们可以预期，在边际上，推理会向最具经济价值的任务倾斜，而其他一切都会被挤出市场。 理论上，在资源极度稀缺的环境下，这会导致推理成本飙升，因为这些任务的价值将远高于今天。 但我认为这种情况不会像文中描述的那样发生，仅仅是因为市场力量会通过竞争来争夺推理需求，从而不断压低价格，直到产能跟上。 有太多模型提供商和基础设施公司想要这些工作负载，以至于不会产生这里所提出的效应。但走着瞧吧！
