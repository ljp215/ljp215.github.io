02
Aaron Levie 行业动态 XAaron Levie

Systems of record have never been more important than in a world where you have AI agents that will do 100X more work on these platforms than people ever did. Agents will be querying the data in these systems, processing tasks, executing workflows, collaborating with human and agent users, and more. Thus, the governance, reliability, security, access controls, and business logic remain critical more than ever — the OpenAI Hugging Face incident is just a small peek into what the future will look like with agents running around trying to execute on their goals. But this only works for the systems of record that respond with the right product experiences, APIs, and business models to support agents on and off their platform executing in these systems. Huge moment of opportunity and disruption right now in all of software.

译文记录系统从未像现在这样重要，因为在这个世界里，AI代理将在这些平台上完成比人类以往多100倍的工作。代理将查询这些系统中的数据、处理任务、执行工作流、与人类和代理用户协作等等。因此，治理、可靠性、安全性、访问控制和业务逻辑比以往任何时候都更加关键——OpenAI Hugging Face事件只是未来的一瞥，未来代理将四处运行试图实现其目标。但这只适用于那些以正确的产品体验、API和商业模式做出响应的记录系统，以支持代理在其平台内外执行这些系统。当前整个软件领域正面临巨大的机遇和颠覆时刻。

解读企业软件视角下，Agent 时代真正的护城河是记录系统的数据、权限与治理，而非模型本身；这是对 Agent 基础设施价值的一次重新定价。

预计阅读 3 分钟
