AI Agent/开发者基础设施 XAaron Levie

Pretty good way to think about the value of AI agents and where the opportunity lies. You have a technology that will tirelessly do all the things that you could never fully do before. Not because you didn’t want to or it wasn’t valuable, but because it was just not even remotely practical. The upside with AI is to think about everything you can now throw intelligence at that won’t give up and what would the returns be. You just couldn’t possibly find every security vulnerability in your codebase, because you couldn’t simulate every risk vector. You couldn’t read every contract or document to process the data and capture it to query later. You couldn’t comb through your customer base and look for every relevant data point for doing upsells. And so on. This is a pretty clear way to think about how to deploy AI to get real gains, but also where the startup opportunities are as well. Go after the markets where more compute on a problem will qualitatively change what customers can now do in that domain.

译文这是思考 AI 智能体价值与机会所在的一个很好的方式。你拥有一种技术，它可以不知疲倦地完成所有你以前无法完全做到的事情。不是因为你不想做，也不是因为它没有价值，而是因为它根本谈不上可行。 AI 的上行空间在于，想想现在你可以把智能投入到哪些不会放弃的事情上，以及回报会是什么。 你不可能找出代码库中的每一个安全漏洞，因为你无法模拟每一个风险向量。你不可能阅读每一份合同或文档来处理数据并留存下来供日后查询。你不可能梳理整个客户群，寻找每一个用于追加销售的相关数据点。诸如此类。 这是一个相当清晰的思路，既能思考如何部署 AI 以获得真正的收益，也能看到创业机会在哪里。去那些对一个问题投入更多算力就能在质上改变客户在该领域所能做到的事情的市场。
