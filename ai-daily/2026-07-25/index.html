AI Agent/开发者基础设施 XAaron Levie

The best way to think about AI is as a force multiplier for the fields you already know about (or for the rate at which you want to learn about a new one). The 3rd category -no existing judgment, no interest in developing it- will basically produce slop and won’t drive that much economically productive activity. What will happen is that the experts will get better and better at their craft and be able to do so much more. Because they can wield these tools in ways that actually drive high quality output, veer agents in the right direction when they go off, and actually incorporate the work into something useful. The expert engineer with agents will do far more productive work, precisely because they know how to steer the agent properly. The designer will product far better outcomes using AI than someone without an eye for design. And so on. As a result of this dynamic, specialization will continue to be important, if not even more so as the tools get more powerful. Because the expectation of the market gets to be much higher. Getting good at any craft will continue to be necessary in the future.

译文理解AI的最佳方式，是将其视为你已知领域的杠杆倍增器（或加速学习新领域的速度）。第三类——既无现有判断力，也无兴趣培养它——基本上只会产出垃圾，不会驱动太多经济生产活动。 实际情况是，专家们会越来越精通自己的技艺，并能够完成更多工作。因为他们能以真正产出高质量成果的方式运用这些工具，在智能体偏离方向时将其拉回正轨，并将工作整合成有用的东西。 拥有智能体的专家工程师将完成远更高效的工作，正因为他们知道如何正确引导智能体。具备设计眼光的设计师使用AI产出的成果，远胜于缺乏设计感的人。以此类推。 这种动态的结果是，专业化将继续重要，甚至随着工具变得更强大而更加重要。因为市场的期望会变得更高。未来，精通任何一门手艺仍将是必要的。
