AI 模型/产品发布 XNikunj Kothari

Maybe I’m just old school, but I just can’t believe how people make investments over a Zoom call.. and sometimes by just meeting the CEO. There’s so much alpha in meeting founders at their own office. Besides “vibes”, you get to see what the energy of the office is like, how the coworkers react and work with each other, how the cofounders answer and complement each other, what the beta product releases look like, how they collaborate etc etc. Yes, I get it there’s time pressure, and you often have to move fast. But even then I always ask to meet at offices for final meetings and founders often tell me I’m the only one that has ever asked. What’s the point of calling them into your own conference room and parrot the same deck??

译文也许我只是老派，但我就是无法相信人们能通过一个 Zoom 通话做出投资决定……有时甚至只是见 CEO 一面就投了。 在创始人自己的办公室见他们，能获得很多 alpha。 除了“氛围”，你还能看到办公室的能量如何，同事们如何互动协作，联合创始人如何回答和互补，beta 产品发布是什么样，他们如何合作等等等等。 是的，我明白有时间压力，你往往得快速行动。但即便如此，我总是要求在办公室进行最终会议，而且创始人常告诉我，我是唯一提出过这个要求的人。 把他们叫进你的会议室，让他们复述同一套演示文稿，到底有什么意义？
