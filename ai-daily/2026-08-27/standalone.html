AI 研究/Benchmark XMadhu Guru

How to build great evals — Part 9 The Eval Roadmap Problem Most evals fail because teams treat them as static artifacts while their users expectations and behaviors have evolved. Your evals need a roadmap that evolves with your product and actual usage patterns. Take a financial research agent. Here is how use cases will evolve over time. Early user: Summarize this 5-page earnings report. 3 weeks later: review the last 5 earnings reports and explain their growth story. 2 months later: Here are 15 filings, earnings transcripts and research reports. Build an investment thesis. Eventually: Monitor my stock portfolio and tell send me alerts when something materially changes my thesis. Each stage requires different capabilities and hence different evals. Your evals should change with usage patterns: short-context -> long-context single-turn QA -> multi-turn passage citations -> doc and line citations Simple QA -> complex synthesis reactive chat -> proactive agent If your evals are stuck in week 1 while users are in week 3, it will show in your product and churn metrics. Here’s a practical way to build the roadmap: 1/ Map the dimensions along which usage will evolve (eg # turns, document size, tool use, autonomy, journey coverage). 2/ Prioritize the use cases and dimensions that matter most for your product. 3/ Talk to your users, mine production traces to look for shifts. 4/ Build P0 evals for the next stage of usage. 5/ Run the evals, find failure modes (remember the taxonomy from Part 3), and hill-climb (from part 6). The goal is to stay ahead of usage patterns. PM 101. Drop your questions and I will address in future posts. Share this with your teammates who think your evals are perfect.

译文如何构建出色的评估——第9部分 评估路线图问题 大多数评估失败是因为团队把它们当作静态工件，而用户的期望和行为已经发生了变化。 你的评估需要一个与产品和实际使用模式同步演进的路线图。 以金融研究智能体为例。用例会如何随时间演变： 早期用户：总结这份5页的财报。 3周后：回顾最近5份财报，解释它们的增长故事。 2个月后：这里有15份文件、财报电话会议记录和研究报告。构建一个投资论点。 最终：监控我的股票投资组合，当有重大变化影响我的论点时，给我发送提醒。 每个阶段都需要不同的能力，因此也需要不同的评估。 你的评估应该随着使用模式而变化： 短上下文 -> 长上下文 单轮问答 -> 多轮 段落引用 -> 文档和行级引用 简单问答 -> 复杂综合 被动聊天 -> 主动智能体 如果你的评估停留在第1周，而用户已经进入第3周，这会在你的产品和流失指标中体现出来。 以下是构建路线图的实用方法： 1/ 映射使用将演进的维度（例如轮数、文档大小、工具使用、自主性、旅程覆盖）。 2/ 优先考虑对你的产品最重要的用例和维度。 3/ 与用户交谈，挖掘生产痕迹以寻找变化。 4/ 为下一阶段的使用构建P0评估。 5/ 运行评估，找出失败模式（记住第3部分的分类法），并进行爬山优化（来自第6部分）。 目标是领先于使用模式。产品经理基础。 有问题尽管提，我会在后续帖子中解答。 把这分享给那些认为你的评估完美无缺的队友吧。
